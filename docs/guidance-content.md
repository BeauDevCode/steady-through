# Life-event guidance content and source notes

The bundled catalog uses this original editorial copy; it is not a validated intervention. Guides are free and usable offline with all learning sources disabled. Source links below are optional and may need internet access. All activities are optional, repeatable, and skippable. A stage is a place to begin, not a prerequisite or emotional target. Standard and simple copy express the same choice. Context selection and helper use are voluntary and need no date of birth. Do not infer age, diagnosis, symptoms, or family safety. Users need not write, save, or share personal details.

Shared standard introduction: Choose any starting point. Try an idea, change it, or skip it. You do not have to write or share personal details.

Shared simple introduction: Pick a place to start. You can skip anything. You do not have to tell your story.

## General

General stage prompts retain the existing Journey screen standard and simple copy verbatim for compatibility. Other guide copy follows the source-informed editorial brief.

Description simple: Think about caring tasks and what you need too.

## Source notes and limits

These six primary sources inform the general ideas below. The wording above is original, not quoted. The sources do not evaluate Steady Through, these exact prompts, the reading level, or the five-stage sequence. Do not claim diagnosis, treatment, clinical efficacy, stress measurement, guaranteed relief, or a required emotional result. No source requires app users to provide a date of birth or disclose their story; avoiding those requirements is a product choice.

1. [NIMH: Caring for Your Mental Health](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health). Supports individually chosen enjoyable activities, priorities, manageable daily tasks, reflection on accomplishments, social contact, and practical help. Used for General and Adult life changes. The page also recommends professional support for concerning symptoms; the guide must not interpret symptoms or impose a waiting period.
2. [UNICEF: Positive Parenting](https://www.unicef.org/sop/positive-parenting). Supports understandable explanations, listening, familiar routines, warm relationships, play, and collaborative problem solving. This is adult-facing parenting guidance; child-facing copy is an editorial adaptation. Do not assume a parent is safe or available.
3. [UNICEF: How to support your teen during stressful times](https://www.unicef.org/parenting/child-care/how-support-your-teen-during-stressful-times). Supports achievable daily goals, enjoyable breaks, routines, collaborative solutions, trusted support, and respect for independence. Does not validate independent app use by young children.
4. [NIH: Take care of yourself while caring for others](https://www.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/social-care-yourself-others-checklist.pdf). Supports organizing tasks, identifying specific help, feasible breaks, and interests outside caregiving; recognizes that options vary by circumstance. The care-covered condition is an implementation safeguard. No medication or hands-on care instructions are included.
5. [NIA: Participating in Activities You Enjoy As You Age](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/healthy-aging/participating-activities-you-enjoy-you-age?linkId=925673184). Supports meaningful activities, connection, adapting to retirement or changed roles, and choosing an activity balance that suits the person. Do not infer loneliness, disability, bereavement, or retirement from age. Full indexed text was retrieved; direct canonical fetch returned HTTP 405, so browser availability may vary.
6. [NIH: Cope With Loss](https://www.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/emotional-wellness-cope-loss-checklist.pdf). Supports supportive contact, patience, and individual mourning experiences. Loss is optional in this context family; reflection must not demand acceptance, progress, closure, or a fixed grief timetable.

Additional design limits: Keep breathing and closed-eye exercises optional if offered elsewhere; none is required here. Do not require gratitude or disclosure. Keep guides usable offline; source links may require internet access. Reflection checks whether an option fits, not whether distress improved. Source links inform the design and do not imply endorsement. Helper support is optional, with trusted adult/person wording rather than an assumed parent. No helpline numbers were researched or supplied.

Research date: 2026-11-06. Five sources opened directly; NIA full content was available through indexed retrieval as described above.
